# Clinical safety
- Intended use: hackathon demonstration and workflow exploration only.
- Not diagnosis, treatment, medical advice, or replacement for a clinician.
- Triage logic is deterministic and separate from the LLM.
- Positive emergency rules take precedence over uncertainty and AI wording.
- Silence/unmentioned symptoms are not negatives. Uncertain ASR must be re-recorded or clarified.
- Current demo rules are illustrative, not a complete WHO/DGHS protocol and not clinically validated.
- A qualified obstetric/maternal-health team must map each rule, threshold, gestational applicability, urgency and wording to current national guidance before deployment.
- Emergency advice should be locally reviewed and include verified referral destinations/contact pathways.
- Do not use this app to delay emergency care.
